How we work

Engagement models built for Zimbabwean business.

We don’t do generic packages — every engagement starts with a short discovery so we can quote against the real scope. The four shapes below cover most of what clients ask for.

Fixed-scope
Project

Defined deliverable, fixed budget, agreed timeline. Best for websites, integrations, and bounded software builds.

Ongoing
Retainer / Support

Monthly capacity for build, maintenance and IT support — SLA-backed, with a named point of contact.

Embedded
Staff augmentation

Senior Clipflow engineers, designers or support specialists join your team for a defined period — full integration, your tools.

Procurement
Hardware supply

Just-In-Time supply of laptops, servers, networking and peripherals (HP, Dell, Lenovo, Epson, Samsung, LG) — procurement-ready paperwork.

Not sure which shape fits? Tell us what you're trying to do and we’ll suggest one.
