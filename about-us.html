Our story

A Zimbabwean technology firm with a global engineering practice.

Our Company Mission
To build transformative software solutions that empower Zimbabwean businesses to operate efficiently, compliantly, and competitively in the digital economy.
Our Company Vision
A Zimbabwe where hyperlocal, purpose-built software enables every business—from SMEs to enterprises—to thrive through seamless integration of compliance, payments, and operational excellence.
Our Philosophy
We believe in solving real problems for real markets. Rather than importing generic solutions, we build deep expertise in Zimbabwe's unique regulatory, payment, and business landscape. Our products are purpose-designed—not adaptations—because local challenges deserve local wisdom. We champion pragmatism, compliance excellence, and sustainable growth.
Our Strategy
  1. Build vertical software solutions for Zimbabwe's regulatory and operational challenges
  2. Integrate seamlessly with local payment and compliance ecosystems (EcoCash, Paynow, ZIMRA)
  3. Maintain reliability and compliance excellence as competitive differentiators
  4. Scale sustainably by solving high-value, recurring problems
